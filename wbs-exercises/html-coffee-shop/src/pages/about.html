A little coffee, a lot of heart.

Common Ground Coffee began with a simple idea: a great neighbourhood coffee shop should make everyone feel welcome.

We care about the details, from choosing quality beans to taking the time to prepare every cup properly. Our menu celebrates good coffee, fresh ingredients, and the small pleasures of taking a break.

More than anything, we want to create a place where neighbours become familiar faces, ideas are shared, and nobody feels they have to rush.
