About Us

At Happy Tails, our mission is to connect wonderful dogs with caring, responsible families. We provide a safe and supportive environment for dogs while they wait for their forever homes.

We believe adoption changes lives. Our team is here to help you find a companion whose personality, needs, and energy are a good match for your home.

Why Adopt From Us?

  • Comprehensive veterinary checks for every dog
  • Behavioural support and basic training guidance
  • Personality and lifestyle matching
  • Honest information about each dog's needs and history
  • Post-adoption advice and support
  • Adoption process focused on the wellbeing of each dog
